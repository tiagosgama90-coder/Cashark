# Cashark Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 5 August 2026

Cashark (“we”, “our”, “the app”) respects your privacy.

## Data we collect

- Account data you provide (email, display name, password hash stored locally on device for demo accounts)
- Gameplay progress (Cash, Sharks, points, lives, spins)
- Advertising identifiers when ads are shown (via Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)
- Basic device information required by the ad SDK

## How we use data

- To operate login, rewards, shop, and cashout requests
- To show rewarded advertisements
- To improve stability and fairness of the game economy

## Third parties

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Their policies apply when ads load.

## Children’s privacy

Cashark is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under the applicable age in your country.

## Cashouts

Virtual Cash and Sharks have no guaranteed real-world value until a cashout is approved under our terms. Minimum cashout is €1.00. Fraudulent activity may void balances.

## Contact

For privacy requests, contact the developer email listed on the Samsung Galaxy Store listing.

## Updates

We may update this policy. Continued use of Cashark after changes means you accept the updated policy.
